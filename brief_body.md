# Meeting Management Index

**Scope and Mode:** Index / Dashboard (Operate)
**Audience:** Faculty, Students, Admins of ICTU
**Job:** Find, book, and manage meeting rooms
**Action/Task:** View room availability in a timetable, book an empty slot
**Proof/Content:** Real-time room status, conflict-free scheduling, equipment lists
**Constraints:** Tech Blue / Deep Navy brand, clear time slots, strict conflict prevention

## Direction contract

THESIS: Rows of times, columns of rooms, a strict high-density grid. The category-default generic dashboard is refused in favor of a specialized, academic timetable ledger.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy and tech blue (#1e3a8a, #0f172a), strict grid lines, color-coded tags. Distinct from generic SaaS, reading like an institutional master schedule.

STORY: The visitor (faculty or student) understands instantly which rooms are free, believes the data is conflict-free, and books directly from the slot without leaving the grid context.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A strict calendar matrix occupying the full screen width. Rooms are columns, times are rows. The primary action is tapping an empty cell to claim it, pulling the booking form into the front plane with a strong depth hierarchy.

FORM: The University Ledger. (Candidate 5 / Seed key b43f2bbc).
